✦How to read the tax line

Use your effective all-in rate — federal + state + city tax actually paid, divided by gross pay — not your top bracket. Check last year's return: total tax ÷ gross income. A no-income-tax state can move this line 5–10 points, which is often the whole decision.

✦Saved Crossings

The method. Salary is a headline; leftover is a life. First, taxes are not a footnote — in a cross-state move they can be worth more than the raise itself, so both cities are taxed at their real effective rate. Second, housing gets no romance: the cheaper city only wins if you actually bank the difference instead of upgrading it away. Third, a move has to pay for its own truck — if the monthly gain cannot retire the moving cost inside about two years, you are not relocating, you are donating. Plot the course before you pack a single box.
